Quick safety checklist

  • Tell a trusted person what is happening.
  • Do not share passwords, OTPs, banking details, or private images.
  • Save relevant evidence securely when it is safe to do so.
  • Secure your accounts and enable two-factor authentication.
  • Report threats through the relevant platform or authorities.
