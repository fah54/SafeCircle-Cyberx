Think before you click. Check suspicious messages for common warning signs.

Your message is checked in this browser using simple warning-sign rules. Avoid pasting passwords, OTPs, bank details, or other private information.

Stay safe online

  • Never share your OTP or password.
  • Do not click links from unexpected messages.
  • Verify urgent payment requests through a trusted channel.
  • Block and report suspicious senders when appropriate.
