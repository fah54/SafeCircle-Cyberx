🛡️ Women's Safety Guide

Simple steps that can help you prepare and stay safer.

  • Tell a trusted person about your travel plans when appropriate.
  • Keep your phone charged and important contacts accessible.
  • Choose a safer public place if you feel threatened.
  • Trust your judgement and seek help when you feel unsafe.
  • Do not put yourself at greater risk to collect evidence.

📚 Digital Safety Centre

Protect your information and online accounts.

  • Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords with strangers.
  • Verify suspicious links before opening them.
  • Use strong, unique passwords for your accounts.
  • Enable two-factor authentication where available.
  • Save evidence of online harassment when safe to do so.
  • Report abusive accounts using the platform's official reporting tools.

💜 You Are Not Alone

If you experience harassment or feel unsafe, consider reaching out to someone you trust or an appropriate support service.

In India, 112 is the emergency number. For women-related assistance, 181 may be available depending on your location.
