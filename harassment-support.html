Online Harassment Support

You deserve to feel safe online. Find practical steps and support options below.

What can you do right now?

1
Prioritize your safety

If you feel in immediate danger, move to a safe place and contact someone you trust or local emergency services.

2
Preserve evidence safely

Where safe and lawful, save screenshots, usernames, profile links, dates, and relevant messages. Store them somewhere secure.

3
Block and report

Use the platform's reporting tools. Consider restricting or blocking the account if it is safe for you to do so.

4
Secure your account

Review privacy settings, change a compromised password from a trusted device, and enable two-factor authentication.

5
Reach out for support

Tell a trusted friend, family member, teacher, or support professional. You do not have to handle harassment alone.

Support in India

Emergency: 112

If you face immediate danger, call India's emergency response number.

Call 112

For cybercrime reporting in India, visit the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. You can also seek help through the appropriate police or cybercrime unit.
